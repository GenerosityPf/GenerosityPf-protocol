# Generosity Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** An internet where the first move is an offer, not an ask.

**Mission.** Build a Solana community that rewards showing up for others: sharing what we know, keeping the tone warm, and doing our work in the open. Generosity is the token and the symbol of that agreement, two open hands shaped as a heart.

## Roadmap

1. **Phase 1, Open the door.** Launch the site, publish the contract address, and bring the first holders in.
2. **Phase 2, Build the habit.** Run community moments, ship the public repository, and publish plain-language updates.
3. **Phase 3, Widen the circle.** Partner with other Solana communities and creators who share the same tone.
4. **Phase 4, Hand over the keys.** Move decisions toward holders with transparent proposals and open records.
